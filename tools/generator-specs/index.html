This is a draft tool to calculate potential emissions from gas- and diesel-fired generation equipment. Emissions factors and calculations are still being validated.

For reference only. These values and estimates are compiled from manufacturer documents, permits, AP-42 and the literature, and many are calculated. They are not guarantees or permit terms. Check every value against the cited source, and verify carefully before using it in any regulatory analysis.
Each row is one source's description of one variant. Values are at the rated / 100% load point. Italic grey values are calculated from the cited inputs; superscripts link to sources below. A small gross / net marks the output basis the source states; * marks a heating-value basis inferred from the numbers and ? one the source doesn't state (shown under both HHV and LHV). Hover a value or a mark for details.

Emission rate per unit uses the variant's lb/MWh × MW/unit when available, else its lb/hr (scaled if you change MW/unit), else lb/MMBtu × heat rate × MW. Grey † CO2 falls back to 40 CFR 98 Table C-1 (116.98 lb/MMBtu gas, 163.05 diesel) × heat input. PM10 and PM2.5 with no value in the database fall back to the technology's AP-42 factor × heat input, marked (AP-42), as permits for gas turbines and engines usually do; a variant without a heat rate uses the median rated heat rate of the model's other variants (see calc). Diesel PM10 / PM2.5 calculated from total PM (all PM10, 97% PM2.5, EPA NR-009d) are marked split in the browse table. A dash = no factor yet: use factors on a row to enter your own (lb/MWh, lb/hr, lb/MMBtu or ppmvd@15% O2), load the AP-42 or a measured literature factor for the technology, or compute SO2 from fuel sulfur. Blue marks user, AP-42 or literature values. CO2e is always computed: CO2 + 29.8 × CH4 + 273 × N2O (IPCC AR6 GWP-100, fossil methane), from the same rates as those columns, including overrides; "(no CH4)" means that term is missing. Generic AP-42 and literature variants start from a typical unit: the median rated size and HHV heat rate of the database's own models of that technology. Replace them with the actual unit's values. Superscripts link to sources below.

Each point is one variant's headline value (rated / 100% load), as in the browse table. Boxes show the median and quartiles; whiskers reach the furthest point within 1.5 × the interquartile range. Hollow points are calculated values. Hover a point for details; click it (or a row below) to select it. Permit limits are not measured emissions, and engine-out and stack values are not comparable: use Color by or Group by to keep them apart.
